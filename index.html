Voice to Text

Live dictation with voice-based correction. Works best in Chrome, Edge, or Safari.

Fix a word: double-click it (highlights), then say the correct word. Drag across several words (or Shift+click) to replace a phrase; the next thing said replaces the highlight and typing returns to the end. Alternatives: single-click a word for similar-sounding options. Commands: say "period", "comma", "question mark", "exclamation point", "colon", "semicolon", "new line", "new paragraph". Say "literal period" to write the word itself. Delete: Backspace or saying "delete that" removes the last word. If a similar-sounding word is said right after, the next alternative is used (likely a mis-hearing), and repeating cycles through the options. Saying "cap that" capitalizes the previous word. Esc cancels a highlight.
